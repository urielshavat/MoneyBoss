When uploading to GitHub via the website:
- Prefer dragging the whole android-app folder
- Especially re-upload these if the widget breaks:
  app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml
  app/src/main/res/layout/widget_balance.xml
  app/src/main/java/com/moneyboss/app/BalanceWidgetProvider.kt
- Do NOT hand-edit Hebrew inside GitHub's editor if your browser mangles encoding
